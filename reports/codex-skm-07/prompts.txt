TAKE 1 / v2 compact tuck
Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset: one horizontal strip containing exactly SIX separate full-body pixel-art animation poses of Xion, same character and scale in all six. This is take v2, compact airborne tuck.
Reference image 1 is the authoritative pixel sprite anchor: copy her face size, bob haircut, black/silver clothes, short coat, two red scarf tails, short twin pump shotguns and pixel detailing exactly. Reference image 2 is costume detail only: ignore the red hologram completely, draw only the foreground black-clothed woman. Reference image 3 is the airborne posture reference: suspended boots, bent knees, loose hanging legs. Use all three references.
Six poses in one left-to-right row, all facing right in the same three-quarter profile as the anchor. Each silhouette is fully visible with wide empty green gaps. Flat pure #00ff00 background everywhere; no ground, shadows, markings, labels or grid. No muzzle flashes, smoke, projectiles, shells, effects or additional figures. Exactly two arms and two legs, and one separate short pump shotgun grasped in EACH hand in EVERY frame.
The sequence is a DOWNWARD AIR SHOT while already airborne. ALL SIX FRAMES ARE FLOATING IN MID-AIR. Never stand, never squat with flat soles on a shared ground plane. Feet dangle/toes point downward, legs bend at the knees. Her torso stays upright, at almost the SAME HEIGHT throughout; firing has only a very small upward nudge. Keep head size unchanged.
Pose 1: mid-air compact tuck; both knees lifted, shins bent with boots dangling above the normal feet line, guns lowered pointing down.
Pose 2: maintain compact tucked knees, extend both arms down along either side of the legs, both distinct gun barrels point vertically straight down below her. Keep the head close to pose 1's height.
Pose 3: the actual recoil! Head and torso rise by only 4 final sprite pixels (about one sixth of her head height) from pose 2. Legs kick OUT AND DOWN into a small open V, soles angled downward and NEVER planted. Boots therefore extend LOWER than in pose 2 despite the small head rise. Guns recoil upward a little but still point down. Scarf tails and coat hem flip upward. Not a standing pose.
Pose 4: head remains at pose 3's height within 2 final pixels, guns still down, knees begin to draw back in; distinct follow-through pose.
Pose 5: recover; guns swing toward the hips, legs hang beneath the torso with softly bent knees and toe-down floating boots.
Pose 6: upright airborne falling pose similar to reference 3's first/last air pose, two guns low, legs hanging, one knee subtly bent, feet never planted.
The scale target after reduction is a 192x192 cell, body centre x96 and a reference feet line y152, but do NOT DRAW any lines. Boots target y141,141,149,144,147,150 respectively. Head rises only 3-5 pixels from pose 2 to 3; do not straighten from a crouch. This strip must visibly read as suspension in air, not a ground squat. Keep anatomy and costume consistent with anchor, crisp detailed pixel art rather than large smooth illustration.
